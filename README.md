# Pathfinder — Find Your Purpose

A free, private, research-backed assessment that helps anyone find a direction for their life.
It walks through the four questions of Ikigai — **what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for** —
and ends with a personal **purpose statement** and a **30-day experiment** to test it in real life.

## The five steps

1. **Ikigai-9** (Imai et al., 2012) — how strongly you feel your life is worth living today, across three dimensions.
2. **Interests (Holland RIASEC)** — 18 activities that reveal your three-letter interest code.
3. **Values & causes** — up to 5 values and up to 3 problems in the world that move you.
4. **Strengths** — up to 5 things that come naturally to you.
5. **Reflection** — your life stage plus optional open questions in your own words.

## What you get

- An editable purpose statement written from your answers
- An Ikigai map of the four circles
- Career directions, roles that serve your causes, and business ideas
- Ways to live your purpose without changing jobs
- A 30-day plan adapted to your life stage (student, career change, caregiver, retired, …)
- A gentle support note with helpline links when answers suggest someone is struggling

Answers are saved only in the browser (`localStorage`); nothing is sent to a server.

## Run locally

It's a static site with no build step:

```sh
python3 -m http.server
# open http://localhost:8000
```

Deploy by enabling GitHub Pages on this repository.
