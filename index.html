            Questions About Finding Purpose

            How do I find my purpose in life?

            Purpose sits where four things overlap: what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for. Notice what energizes you, name your strengths, choose a cause you care about, then test small experiments for 30 days. Purpose is built through action more than found by thinking.

            What is ikigai?

            Ikigai (生き甲斐) is a Japanese word meaning "reason for being" — the sense that your life is worth living and that you matter to someone or something. Researchers measure it with the Ikigai-9 scale (Imai et al., 2012).

            What should I do when I feel lost in life?

            Feeling lost is a normal starting point. Start small: track which activities give you energy, talk to people whose lives interest you, and try one low-risk experiment a week. If the feeling comes with lasting sadness or hopelessness, talk to a doctor, counselor or a helpline.

            Do I need to change my job to live my purpose?

            No. Purpose can be lived through volunteering, caregiving, a side project, mentoring, or reshaping your current role toward the parts that use your strengths for people you care about.
